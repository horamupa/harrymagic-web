What is Harry Magic: Potions Class?

Harry Magic: Potions Class is a potion making game for iPhone and iPad. You play a first-year at a wizard school. Each class gives you a recipe in exact parts and one minute to pour it into the cauldron, and the Professor gives you a mark. There are ten classes, eight potions and one final exam.

How a class works

  1. Pour the recipe

    Lift a vial with one finger and tip it over the cauldron. Love Potion takes 60% Nightshade, 30% Mermaid Tears and 10% Dragon Blood, and you judge the layers by eye.

  2. Mind the toads and the fire

    Toads hop into your potion, so swipe them out. Tap the salamander under the cauldron when the fire cools down.

  3. Earn your mark

    Pour it right and the Professor says "Superb! Not a drop spilled." Pour it wrong and he says "That's not a potion, it's soup. Start over."

Mixology: learn to mix colors

Between potion classes you mix red, yellow and blue paint drop by drop until the bottle matches the potion's color. Match it and you get the recipe. A table on the parchment shows what each pair of colors makes.

Made for fans of wizard school stories

If the potions classes were your favorite part of Harry Potter, this game puts you at the bench with the cauldron, the vials and a strict Professor. Sixteen medals hang in the hall, three of them secret. Harry Magic is an independent game with its own recipes, and it has no connection to the Harry Potter books or films.

Questions

Is Harry Magic free?

The download is free and so are the first six classes, three Potions and three Mixology. One purchase opens the full course. In the US it costs $2.99 once. There are no ads and no subscription.

Is this an official Harry Potter game?

No. Harry Magic is an independent game. It is not connected to Warner Bros., Portkey Games or J.K. Rowling. It is made for people who enjoy wizard school stories.

What age is it for?

The App Store rates it 4+. The only thing to read is the recipe, and one finger is enough to play.

What does the game teach?

Percentages and proportions, because every recipe is written in exact parts. Mixology classes teach how colors mix. Both also train attention.

Does it need the internet?

No, you can play without a connection. The app goes online for the purchase, for anonymous analytics and crash reports, and for feedback you choose to send.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 18 or later. It is available in 35 languages.
