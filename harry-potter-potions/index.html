Potions in Harry Potter: what each one does

The potions in Harry Potter range from a quick cold cure to a luck potion that takes six months to brew. The best known are Polyjuice Potion, Felix Felicis, Amortentia and Veritaserum. Here is what each one does and where it matters in the story.

Who teaches potions at Hogwarts?

Severus Snape teaches Potions for Harry's first five years, in a cold dungeon classroom. Horace Slughorn takes over in the sixth year, when Snape moves to Defence Against the Dark Arts.

The two teachers feel very different. Snape picks on Harry and Neville, and Slughorn collects favorite students. Harry's marks jump under Slughorn, thanks to a second-hand textbook full of notes from its old owner.

What is the hardest potion in Harry Potter?

Polyjuice Potion is the one the books spend the most time on. It takes about a month to brew, and the ingredients are hard to get, so Hermione has to take some from Snape's private stores.

Felix Felicis is harder still. Slughorn says it takes six months to brew and goes badly wrong if you make a mistake.

What does a potion need to work?

Exact amounts and exact order. In the Potions classroom a recipe is a list of steps, and one wrong ingredient can melt the cauldron, as Neville learns in his first lesson.

That's the part we built a game around. In Harry Magic: Potions Class every recipe is written in exact parts, you pour by eye, and you have one minute before the Professor looks into the cauldron.

Questions about Harry Potter potions

What is the most powerful potion in Harry Potter?

The books call Amortentia the most powerful love potion in the world. For sheer effect, many fans would pick Felix Felicis, and the Elixir of Life keeps its drinker from dying.

What does Polyjuice Potion do?

It turns you into someone else for about an hour. You add a piece of that person, usually a hair, and the potion changes color and taste to match.

What potion is liquid luck?

Felix Felicis. Whoever drinks it has a run of good luck for as long as it lasts, and Slughorn warns that too much of it is dangerous.

How many potions are there in Harry Potter?

There's no single official count. The books and films name dozens, and fan wikis list more than a hundred with the ones from games and companion books.
