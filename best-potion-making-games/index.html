The best potion making games for iPhone and iPad

A good potion making game on iPhone lets you mix something by hand and see it go right or wrong. These eight do that in different ways. One is a timed class with exact recipes, two are busy potion shops, one is a card game and the rest lean on idle play or free experiments.

We make Harry Magic, so it's first on this list. Judge it like the rest. The other seven come from other developers and nobody paid to be here. We picked games where mixing or brewing is part of the play, and each one was on sale in the US App Store in November 2026.

  1. Harry Magic: Potions Class

    Timed recipesFree, full course $2.99★ 5.0 (5)

    You're a first-year in a wizard school potions class. Each recipe comes in exact parts, and you pour it by eye with one finger before the minute runs out. Love Potion is 10% Dragon Blood, 30% Mermaid Tears and 60% Nightshade. The first six classes are free, and there are no ads.

    Download on the App Store

  2. Mystical Mixing

    Magic craftingFree★ 4.8 (92K)

    You drop mystery ingredients into a cauldron, stir, tap with a wand and get a magic pet. The spell book holds the recipes, and the store page lists love potions, rainbow magic and invisibility among its tricks. You also build a village in a mystical forest. Rated 12+.

    View on the App Store

  3. Potion Punch 2

    Time managementFree★ 4.7 (6.3K)

    A fast restaurant game with a magic theme. You mix colors to brew potions at the Potion Café, cook with baby dragons and serve heroes through hundreds of levels. Potions are one station among several here, so pick it if you like the rush more than the recipe.

    View on the App Store

  4. Potion Punch

    Time managementFree★ 4.8 (1.9K)

    The first game runs closer to the subject. You own a potion shop, mix colors to fill orders across the spectrum and serve elves, dwarves and orcs in seven locations. The store page promises you'll learn some color theory along the way.

    View on the App Store

  5. Miracle Merchant

    Card gameFree★ 4.4 (1.7K)

    A solitaire-style card game where you're apprentice to a master alchemist. You combine ingredient cards to brew what each customer wants. A run takes two or three minutes, the deck changes every game and there are 41 potions to collect. English only.

    View on the App Store

  6. Magic Mushrooms - Idle Game

    IdleFree★ 4.9 (18K)

    Turn mushrooms into potions to cure the world. This one is an idle game, so you unlock stranger mushrooms and power-ups rather than mix by hand. Pick it if you want progress to tick along while you do something else.

    View on the App Store

  7. Idle Magic Academy

    Idle tycoonFree★ 4.8 (25K)

    You run a magic academy instead of attending one. Students take classes in casting spells and brewing potions from herbs, and you expand the school and collect ingredients. It keeps earning coins while you're offline.

    View on the App Store

  8. Little Alchemy 2

    Free experimentFree★ 4.6 (11K)

    No cauldron and no recipes to follow. You combine items to discover new ones and slowly build the world from scratch. It's the choice for players who like the mixing and don't want a timer.

    View on the App Store

Ratings: US App Store, November 2026.

Questions about potion games

What is the best potion making game for iPhone?

It depends on what you want from it. For exact recipes against a timer, try Harry Magic: Potions Class. For a busy shop, Potion Punch. For a short card puzzle, Miracle Merchant.

Is Potion Craft on iPhone?

Not yet. Potion Craft already sells on PC, consoles and Android. Its iOS version is on the US App Store as a pre-order with a release date of January 17, 2027, so we left it off this list for now.

Are there free potion games for iPhone?

Yes. Every game on this list is free to download. Harry Magic has one optional purchase for the full course, and several of the others offer in-app purchases.

Which potion game is good for kids?

Check the age rating on each store page. Harry Magic, Potion Punch, Potion Punch 2, Magic Mushrooms and Idle Magic Academy are rated 4+.
